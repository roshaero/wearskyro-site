THE SKY-WORLD

Every print comes from one sky

Puffy clouds with sleepy faces. A fox with a kite. A falcon on patrol. Jet streams, aurora and contour maps of mountains. The Sky-World is one imaginary place, drawn three ways for three ages.

No stock clip art and no borrowed characters: every design is drawn for SKYRO.

HOW IT'S MADE

Built to be played in

Printed into the fabric

Dye-sublimation prints go into the polyester itself. No film or feel on the skin, and colours that stay bright wash after wash.

Soft interlock polyester

Smooth, light and quick to dry. Elastic waist, flat decorative cord and tag-free neck labels.

Matching sets

Tee or tank with shorts or skort, designed together so getting dressed is one decision.

Checked before it ships

Every design goes through a sample, a fit check and a wash test before bulk production starts.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Easy returns, no stress

Kids grow fast and sizes can be tricky. If the set is not right, we will make it right.

7 days to return

Ask for a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. The set must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags and labels attached.

Free size exchange

Wrong size? Your first size exchange on an order is free, subject to stock. If your size is out, we refund you.

Damaged or wrong item

If your set arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, email us with a photo within 7 days. We replace it or refund you in full, and we pay the return shipping.

Refunds

After we receive and check the return, your refund goes to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

How to return: email hello@wearskyro.com with your order number, the set and size, and the reason. We send you the steps and a pickup or return address.

Not returnable: sets that are worn, washed, altered or missing tags, and anything bought on a final-sale offer that was marked as such at purchase.

Return shipping: if you return a set for a change of mind or size, you pay the return shipping, except for your first size exchange. We will confirm the exact charge before any pickup.

Questions or first-drop news

Ask about sizes, orders or bulk and wholesale. Email us and we will reply. Want new-print news first? Ask to join the launch list.
