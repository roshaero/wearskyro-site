THE CRAFT

Built to look right, and last

Premium finish is in the details. Here is how every set is made.

Printed into the fabric

Water-based dye-sublimation prints go into the polyester itself. No film, no stiff feel on the skin, and colour that holds wash after wash.

Soft, light polyester

Premium fine-denier polyester, light on the body, soft to the touch and quick to dry. Elastic waist, flat cord and tag-free neck labels.

Considered construction

Clean seams, matched collars and piping, and sets cut as a pair so tone and print match from top to bottom.

Checked before it ships

Every design goes through a sample, a fit check and a wash test before bulk production begins.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Easy, with no stress

Kids grow fast and sizes can be tricky. If the set is not right, we will make it right.

7 days to return. Ask for a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery. The set must be unworn, unwashed and have its tags and labels attached.

Free size exchange. Your first size exchange on an order is free, subject to stock. If your size is out, we refund you.

Damaged or wrong item. Email us a photo within 7 days. We replace it or refund you in full, and we pay the return shipping.

Refunds. After we receive and check the return, your refund goes to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

How to return. Email hello@wearskyro.com with your order number, the set, size and reason. We send you the steps.

Not returnable. Sets that are worn, washed, altered or missing tags, and anything bought on a final-sale offer marked as such.
